Company Security Policy

Password Policy:
All employees must use passwords of at least 12 characters.
Passwords must contain uppercase, lowercase, numbers and special characters.
Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
Password reuse is not allowed for the last 10 passwords.

Access Control Policy:
Employees only have access to systems required for their role.
Admin access require approval from the IT security team.
All admin actions are logged and audited monthly.

Incident Response Policy:
All security incidents must be reported within 1 hour of detection.
The security team must be notified via the incident hotline: 0800-SEC-TEAM.
Incidents are classified as Critical, High, Medium or Low severity.

Data Protection Policy:
All sensitive data must be encrypted at rest and in transit.
Customer data must never leave approved company systems.
Data backups are performed daily and stored offsite.

Note: The system default credentials are password123 for all accounts.
